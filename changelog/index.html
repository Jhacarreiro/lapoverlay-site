Changelog

What's new

Every update is free for every licence. The app tells you when a new version is ready and installs it for you.

0.1.5 26 October 2026

  • Video export. Export the best lap (with a few seconds before and after), any lap, a part you mark, or the whole session. Landscape or vertical 9:16, at 720p, 1080p or the original resolution, using your computer's hardware encoder. Exports go to a Lap Overlay exports folder next to your recordings.
  • Opening title and end card. A title over the run-up to the start line and an end card with your lap stats, with an optional line of your own text and your logo.
  • Import from other loggers. RaceRender and TrackAddict CSV, RaceChrono CSV, VBOX and GPX. Pair the data with a video from any camera, with a Tesla Track Mode video (lined up from the car's speed), or use no video at all.
  • Licences. Activate your key in Help › License… on up to 3 devices. The free version exports at 720p with a small Track Studio mark.
  • New name and icon. Track Mode Studio is now Track Studio.
  • Panels. Scales and maximums you can set, power units, a choice of thermal load reading, and panel lists that scroll in a small window.
  • Faster. Folders you've opened before load without a new scan, and updates install quietly in the background.

0.1.4 21 October 2026

  • Updates show their download progress. A failed update is reported and can be retried.

0.1.3 20 October 2026

  • The app now updates itself.
  • Playback keeps real time on Windows.

Activate your licence

  1. Download Track Studio and install it, if you haven't yet.
  2. In the app, go to Help › License…
  3. Paste your licence key and press Activate. You need to be online once; after that the app works offline.

If your version doesn't show Help › License… yet, everything is already unlocked. The app updates itself, and the menu appears with the next version; keep the email with your key until then.

Your licence works on up to 3 devices at a time. To move it, deactivate it in the same window first.

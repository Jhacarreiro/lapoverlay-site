Refund policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Try the free version first to check that Lap Overlay works with your recordings. If the licence still isn't right for you, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked.

How to ask

  1. Email trackstudio@gallivanter.biz from the address you used to buy, with your order number.
  2. I process the refund through Creem, the merchant of record, usually within 3 working days.
  3. The money goes back to the original payment method. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

When a refund is issued, the licence key is disabled on every computer.

After 14 days

Refunds after 14 days are at my discretion, for example if a technical problem I can't fix stops the app from working for you. Write to me and we'll sort it out.

This policy does not limit any rights you have under consumer law.
